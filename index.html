Do you provide home sample collection in Satna?

Yes! We offer convenient home sample collection service across Satna. Book via WhatsApp or call us at 8605506663.

How can I download my test reports?

You can download your reports online through our Download Report section. We also provide physical reports at the lab.

Is fasting required for blood tests?

Fasting requirements vary by test. Typically 8-12 hours fasting is needed for glucose, lipid profile tests. Check specific test requirements on our Services page.

How long does it take to get test results?

Most routine tests are ready within 24 hours. Some specialized tests may take 2-3 days. We notify you once reports are ready.

Are you NABL certified?

Yes, Central Pathology is NABL certified, ensuring the highest standards of quality and accuracy in all our diagnostic services.
