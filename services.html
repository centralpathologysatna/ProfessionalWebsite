Our Services

Basic Full Body Check-up

Complete health screening including CBC, blood sugar, lipid profile, kidney and liver function tests.

Includes :
  • CBC
  • ESR
  • FBS
  • PPBS
  • HbA1c
  • Lipid Profile
  • LFT (Bilirubin, SGOT, SGPT, ALP, Protein)
  • KFT (Urea, Creatinine, Uric Acid)
  • Urine R/M
  • TSH
+ Show all
⏱ Report : 24 hours
⚕ Preparation : Fasting 8-10 hours
